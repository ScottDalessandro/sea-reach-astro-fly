# Sea Reach Competitive Analysis: Point Pleasant Beach Summer Rentals

*Research Date: February 2026*

---

## Market Overview

- **517 active vacation rental listings** in Point Pleasant Beach
- **59-60% average occupancy** across all seasons (top properties hit 75-80% in peak summer)
- **$656-658 average daily rate** market-wide
- **$32,500-$32,900** average monthly revenue per listing
- Platform split: **41% VRBO, 27% Airbnb, 32% both**
- 4-bedroom homes represent **18% of inventory** (~93 listings)
- 98% of PPB listings are entire homes (not rooms)

## Beach Proximity Impact on Pricing

- Properties 1-2 blocks from beach: **20-50% rate premium** over 4+ block homes
- Ocean view adds **34% higher RevPAR** and **32% higher ADR**
- Direct beach access properties: $700-950/night for 3BR
- 2-3 blocks: $500-650/night for 3BR
- 4-5 blocks: $400-500/night for 3BR

**Sea Reach at 1.5 blocks sits in the premium proximity band.**

## Platform & Booking Trends

- **Airbnb:** 85% of US vacation stays, but only 27% of PPB listings
- **VRBO:** Dominates PPB with 41% of listings; stronger for whole-home family rentals
- **Direct bookings:** 34% of vacation rentals nationally (and growing)
- **Mobile bookings:** 55% of all vacation rental bookings
- **Average booking lead time:** 75 days (July bookings: 90 days)
- **Winter lead time:** Only 13 days

---

## Market Trends for 2026

1. **PPB median home price: $1,491,500** (+33% YoY) — drives rental demand from non-owners
2. **2025 summer was weather-disrupted** — pent-up demand likely for 2026
3. **Shoulder season growing** — more travelers booking May/June and September
4. **Direct booking rising** — 34% and growing, platforms losing share
5. **Shorter stays trending** — guests shifting toward extended weekends vs. full weeks
6. **Top properties cover mortgage with summer alone** — $66,000-69,000 gross from 14-week summer season
